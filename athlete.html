Eliud Kipchoge

Kipchoge is the most decorated marathon runner in history and the only person to have covered the distance under two hours, in an unofficial exhibition in Vienna in 2019

What is more interesting is what he is doing now. At 41 he carried the Olympic flag at the opening ceremony of the Winter Games in Milano Cortina, which he described as strange for a man used to asphalt rather than ice. He is running a marathon on every continent across two years, a project tied to his foundation's work on education access.

Eliud Kipchoge

He opened it in Cape Town in May 2026, his first official marathon on African soil. In July he finished twelfth in Porto Alegre, nearly ten minutes behind the winner.

He is losing races in public, on purpose, in front of everyone, in order to reach people who will never run one. That is harder than winning.

Pull quote: “I've been chasing the records, chasing medals, chasing the fastest times.”
